Trupanion is one of the most recognized names in pet insurance. Starting today, it integrates with Digitail. Clinics using Digitail can now submit Trupanion insurance claims, request pre-approvals, send Exam Day Offers to uninsured clients, and receive Trupanion payments directly to their invoices, all without leaving Digitail where the rest of the visit happens.
